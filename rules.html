Play fair

Rules

These rules keep Infernium safe, fair and enjoyable for everyone.

General conduct

  1. Respect all players and staff members.
  2. No harassment, hate speech, threats or discrimination.
  3. Do not impersonate staff or other players.
  4. Follow staff instructions during investigations.
  5. Keep personal information private.

Chat and advertising

  1. No spam, excessive caps or repeated messages.
  2. No advertising other servers or communities.
  3. Do not provoke arguments or create unnecessary drama.
  4. Use the correct channels for reports and support.

Gameplay

  1. No hacked clients, x-ray, macros or unfair modifications.
  2. No exploiting bugs, duplication glitches or unintended mechanics.
  3. No griefing protected builds or bypassing protections.
  4. No alternate-account abuse or punishment evasion.
  5. Do not intentionally create lag machines.

PvP and teams

  1. No combat logging or exploiting safe zones.
  2. Do not scam through staff impersonation.
  3. Team leaders are responsible for managing trusted members.
  4. Agreements between players are not always enforceable by staff.

Bugs and reports

  1. Report serious bugs privately through Discord.
  2. Provide screenshots, video or logs when possible.
  3. Do not publicly accuse players without evidence.
  4. Never abuse a bug before reporting it.

Punishments

  1. Punishments depend on severity and previous offences.
  2. Staff may issue warnings, mutes, kicks or bans.
  3. Appeals must be honest and respectful.
  4. Senior staff decisions are final.

Rise from the ashes. Build your legacy. Rule Infernium.
