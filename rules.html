Play Fair

Network Rules

By playing on Infernium Network, you agree to follow these rules.

01

Respect Everyone

No harassment, hate speech, threats, discrimination or targeted toxicity.

02

No Cheating

Hacked clients, unfair modifications, macros and automation are prohibited.

03

No Exploiting

Do not abuse bugs, glitches, duplication methods or unintended mechanics.

04

Keep Chat Clean

Avoid spam, excessive caps, inappropriate content and disruptive behavior.

05

No Advertising

Advertising other servers, communities or unrelated services is not allowed.

06

Protect Player Experience

Scamming, malicious griefing and attempts to ruin gameplay are forbidden.

07

Follow Staff Instructions

Cooperate with staff members during investigations and moderation actions.

08

No Ban Evasion

Using alternate accounts to bypass a punishment may lead to further action.

09

Do Not Impersonate

Do not pretend to be a staff member, content creator or another player.

10

Use Common Sense

Rules cannot cover every situation. Choose fair, respectful behavior.

Important Notice

Staff may act on behavior that harms the server or community even when it is not listed word-for-word above. Punishments depend on severity and previous offenses.
